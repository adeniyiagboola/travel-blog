Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the world's most famous landmarks. Located in Paris, France, it was completed in 1889 and attracts millions of visitors every year because of its remarkable architecture and breathtaking city views.

Colosseum

The Colosseum in Rome is one of the greatest monuments of the ancient Roman Empire. It was used for gladiator contests and public spectacles and remains one of Italy's most visited historical attractions.

Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty stands proudly in New York Harbor. It symbolizes freedom and democracy and has welcomed immigrants arriving in the United States for over a century.
